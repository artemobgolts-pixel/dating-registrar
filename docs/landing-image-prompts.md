# Изображения интерактивного лендинга

Шесть фотографий сгенерированы встроенным генератором изображений (не скриптом),
затем сохранены в WebP для карточек лендинга. Ниже приведены точные финальные
промпты, чтобы галереи можно было воспроизвести или развить в том же направлении.

## `landing-anticafe-1.webp`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: first image in a landing-page event-card gallery
Primary request: atmospheric establishing photograph of a cozy contemporary anticafe on Nevsky Prospekt in Saint Petersburg, prepared for a relaxed board-game evening with friends
Scene/backdrop: inviting historic-city interior with warm wood, upholstered seating, shelves of board games, teapot and cups, subtle tall windows; no identifiable business branding
Subject: the room is the hero; a small group of five adult friends appears naturally in the midground, candid and unposed, faces not prominent
Style/medium: photorealistic editorial lifestyle photography with real fabric and wood texture, subtle film grain
Composition/framing: wide landscape composition, eye level, central visual interest and generous crop-safe margins for responsive 16:10 and portrait crops
Lighting/mood: warm practical lamps mixed with soft early-evening window light; sociable, calm, tasteful
Color palette: warm amber and ivory with restrained indigo and teal accents matching a sophisticated standard theme
Constraints: no text, no logos, no trademarks, no watermark, no UI, no surreal elements, no exaggerated luxury
Avoid: staged stock-photo smiles, visible readable signage, fisheye distortion, oversaturation
```
